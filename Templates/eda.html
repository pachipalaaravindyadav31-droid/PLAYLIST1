Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Explore the statistical distributions, correlations, and feature relationships from the Spotify Tracks dataset.

Audio Features Correlation Heatmap

Key Insights:

  • Energy & Loudness have a strong positive correlation ($r \approx 0.76$), reflecting that louder tracks are perceived as more energetic.
  • Acousticness & Energy exhibit a strong negative correlation ($r \approx -0.73$), as acoustic songs generally possess lower electrical amplification and energy.
  • Popularity shows weak linear correlations with individual features, indicating that track popularity is a complex, non-linear function of combined features, artist branding, and genres rather than a single audio metric.
Danceability vs Energy vs Skip Probability

Key Insights:

Tracks with low danceability and low energy (bottom-left quadrant) are heavily colored in yellow/green, showing a high probability of being skipped. In contrast, tracks with high danceability and energy (top-right quadrant) are colored purple, showing a much lower skip probability. This confirms the validity of our synthetic skip proxy: tracks that lack rhythm and energy are prone to active skipping behavior.

Popularity Distribution

The popularity distribution exhibits a heavily right-skewed pattern with a massive spike at popularity = $0$. This indicates that a large fraction of tracks in the dataset have zero popularity (e.g. local uploads, extremely niche contents). For regression models, this zero-inflation is a major challenge and contributes to the relatively lower $R^2$ scores across all models.

Engineered Skip Probability Proxy

The engineered skip probability score is centered around $0.35$. Applying thresholds divides tracks into risk categories: Low Risk (< 0.35), (0.35 - 0.60) Medium Risk, and High Risk (>= 0.60).

Skip-Risk Category Distribution

Approximately 57.1% of tracks are classified as Low Skip Risk, 37.8% as Medium Skip Risk, and 5.1% as High Skip Risk. This class distribution matches active playlist listening sessions where the majority of songs are kept, a subset is tolerated, and a small, highly incongruent portion is skipped immediately.
